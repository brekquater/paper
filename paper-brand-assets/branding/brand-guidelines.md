# Paper — quick brand guidelines

## Core idea
Paper is a document editing engine. The visual mark should read as a page in the act of being edited: folded page corner, text lines, and an insertion caret.

## Personality
Quiet, precise, useful, developer-oriented. Prefer typographic hierarchy and whitespace over decorative treatments.

## Logo hierarchy
1. Full lockup: use for website headers, launch pages, README hero areas, and presentations.
2. Wordmark: use where the descriptor is redundant or space is constrained.
3. Mark: use for favicon, app tile, compact navigation, and product UI.

## Color
Use charcoal on warm white for the default identity. Use white on near-black for dark surfaces. Day mode uses pure white. Avoid adding a bright accent to the logo itself; product UI may define its own interaction colors separately.

## Exclusion zone and sizing
Keep clear space around the full logo of at least 1× the icon's visible stroke width on each side; 4× is preferred. Full lockup: recommended minimum width 240 px. Mark: recommended minimum 16 px, with 24 px preferred for UI.

## Do not
- Add gradients, glow, bevels, drop shadows, or 3D extrusion.
- Distort proportions or independently resize the icon and wordmark.
- Put the dark logo on a dark background or the light logo on a light background.
- Recreate the wordmark with a different weight or decorative typeface.
- Add “AI” or other product claims to the logo.
